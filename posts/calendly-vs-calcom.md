# Calendly vs Cal.com Free: paid vs free booking links

*21 Nov 2026 · Prices as of 21 Nov 2026, from the official pricing pages. Prices change; check the links before you buy.*

## The quick answer

- **Solo consultant, accountant or freelancer?** Start with **Cal.com Free** ($0, 1 user, unlimited links and calendars, Stripe & PayPal payments).
- **Team already on Calendly, or you need CRM routing?** Stay on **Calendly** (Standard or Teams).
- **Want to run it on your own server?** **Cal.diy** (MIT) exists, but its README says it is for personal, non-production use.

## Plans side by side

| | Calendly (paid) | Cal.com (free tier) |
|---|---|---|
| Free plan | Free: 1 event type, 1 calendar connection | Individuals: $0 "free forever", 1 user |
| Entry paid plan | Standard: $10 per seat / month (billed yearly) | Teams: $12 per user / month (yearly) |
| Next plan | Teams: $16 per seat / month (billed yearly) | Organizations: $28 per user / month (yearly) |
| Enterprise | Starts at $15k / year | Custom (annual) |
| Payments | Stripe, PayPal from Standard | Stripe & PayPal on the free plan |
| Apps | HubSpot, Mailchimp, Zapier from Standard | 100+ app integrations on the free plan |
| Switching | — | "1-click import Calendly events" |

Calendly also has a "Billed monthly" option; monthly prices were not shown on the page we checked, so only yearly prices are listed here.

## Self-host option: Cal.diy

- Repo: https://github.com/calcom/cal.diy (MIT licence, ~50.3k stars, last push 13 Nov 2026, checked 21 Nov 2026)
- The old `calcom/cal.com` repo now redirects here.
- Its README describes it as a community edition for personal, non-production use and recommends Cal.com for commercial use. No Teams, Workflows or SSO.
- Only choose it if you can run, update and secure your own server.

## Who should pick which

- **Calendly:** teams already using it, Salesforce/HubSpot routing, anyone who wants zero change.
- **Cal.com Free:** one person who needs paid bookings and many booking links at $0.
- **Cal.diy:** tinkerers who want full control, personal use only.
