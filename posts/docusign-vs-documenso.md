Notes
- DocuSign's page shows annual-commitment prices; monthly-billed prices were not shown on 25 Nov 2026. No free plan is listed (a free trial is offered).
- documenso/documenso on GitHub: public, AGPL-3.0, ~15.9k stars, last updated 25 Nov 2026. AGPL-3.0: if you change the code and let others use it over a network, you must share your changes.
- Self-hosting guide: https://docs.documenso.com/docs/self-hosting

## Who should pick which
- Freelancer or consultant with a few contracts a month: **Documenso Free**.
- Small team of up to 5: **Documenso Teams** ($40/month).
- Need bulk send or payment collection on signing: **DocuSign Business Pro**.
- Clients already expect DocuSign: **DocuSign Standard** or higher.
- IT team that can run and secure a server: **self-hosted Documenso**.

## Before you sign anything
1. Count last year's contracts — that sets your plan.
2. Count senders — per-user prices grow with each seat.
3. Check e-signature rules for your contract type and country.
4. Test with one demo NDA on a free plan first.
